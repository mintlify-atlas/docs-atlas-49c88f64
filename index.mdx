AI Smart Glasses is an open-source, Linux-based smart glasses platform designed for developers building medical, industrial, educational, and consumer AR applications. The platform runs on a Rockchip RV1106B (ARM Cortex-A7) SoC and exposes hardware capabilities — camera, GPIO, audio, and display — through a clean C SDK backed by prebuilt libraries.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your development environment">
    Clone the [AIGLASS_DEV_ENV](https://github.com/makevary/AIGLASS_DEV_ENV) workspace and run `./setup_build_env.sh` to install the ARM cross-compilation toolchain and all build dependencies on Ubuntu/Debian.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build or flash firmware">
    Run `./build.sh` to compile firmware for your device. If you have the official kit, it ships with factory firmware ready to use — flashing is only needed when you modify the firmware.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your first application">
    Use the [C SDK](/sdk/introduction) to subscribe to GPIO events, capture images, play audio, or render to the display. Cross-compile with the ARM toolchain and push your binary via ADB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and iterate">
    Push your binary with `adb push`, run it on the device, and inspect logs live. The host-side workflow lets you edit, compile, and deploy in seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>
